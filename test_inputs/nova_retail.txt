Nova Retail Ltd. -- Q1 FY26 Financial Summary

Sector: Apparel & Lifestyle Retail
Report Date: 15th July, 2025
Rating: BUY
Target Price: Rs. 890
Current Market Price: Rs. 742
Expected Return: 20%

Headline: Store expansion and online mix drive margin recovery

Company Overview:
Nova Retail Ltd. is a multi-brand apparel and lifestyle retailer operating over
420 stores across India, alongside a fast-growing direct-to-consumer online
channel. The company sources and distributes both in-house and licensed brands.

Key Highlights:
- Consolidated revenue grew 24.6% YoY in Q1FY26 to Rs. 1,842cr, led by same-store
  sales growth of 11.2% and 38 new store additions during the quarter.
- Online channel revenue rose 41.3% YoY to Rs. 312cr, now 17% of total sales.
- Gross margin expanded 180bps YoY to 52.4% on better full-price sell-through and
  reduced discounting.
- EBITDA margin improved to 14.1% from 11.8% in Q1FY25, aided by operating leverage.
- Reported PAT rose 62% YoY to Rs. 94cr.
- Net debt reduced to Rs. 210cr from Rs. 340cr a year ago on stronger operating
  cash flow.

Outlook & Valuation:
Nova Retail's continued store rollout, improving online contribution, and margin
discipline support a positive medium-term outlook. Given the recent re-rating in
the sector, we maintain our BUY rating with a revised target price of Rs. 890,
based on 28x FY27E EPS.
